# ERN Principles

1. **See before you go.**
2. **Camera is the window; Earth is the product.**
3. Keep the mature ERN experience; fix engineering underneath.
4. Quality over quantity on the visible experience.
5. Broad source network underneath; curated simplicity above.
6. Play where clicked; no forced Hero scrolling.
7. One player architecture.
8. Living Atlas broad; Watch Earth selective.
9. Offer multiple good windows because different views become different visitors' favorites.
10. Public/legal sources only. ERN is a bridge/curator and does not host third-party streams.
11. Never silently upgrade a source label.
12. External-only sources open at the provider when embedding is not permitted/reliable.
13. Favorites begin local-first; avoid unnecessary backend complexity.
14. ERN remains alive and adaptable after publication.
