# Earth Signals architecture

Earth Signals are ERN's future ephemeral visitor-evidence layer: a small structured way for people who are at a place to report what is happening **now** without turning ERN into a social network.

## Evidence chain

1. **Structured observation** — approved types such as busy, peaceful, raining or beautiful light.
2. **Place association** — every signal belongs to a known ERN place.
3. **Optional proximity evidence** — **Near this place ✓** requires explicit location permission and a successful near-place check; exact coordinates are never public.
4. **Ephemeral state** — signals expire after 45 minutes.
5. **Presentation** — relative age and expiry remain visible; expired observations disappear.
6. **ERN Guide** — may summarize only as visitor reporting, never as verified camera fact.
7. **Earth is happening** — may aggregate only real, unexpired signals; an empty network stays visibly empty.

## Media

Future photos are temporary; future clips are capped at 15 seconds. Media is disabled by default, must have metadata stripped, storage expiry, and moderation approval, and cannot upgrade a camera's truth status.

## Activation

Contribution stays read-only until all six capabilities are real: submission transport, rate limiting, moderation, reporting, automatic expiry/deletion, and an updated privacy notice.

## Product boundary

No usernames are required, and the initial system has no followers, direct messages, public profiles, free-text comments, likes, permanent post history or public precise contributor location.

The design goal is presence rather than popularity: **someone else on Earth is seeing this moment too.**
