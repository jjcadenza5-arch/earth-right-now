# Remaining historical recovery candidates

After the 2026-09-23 official-source recovery tranche, three historical candidates remain intentionally outside the active catalog.

- **Volcán Tajogaite** — current source and third-party permission need fresh verification. Historical embed evidence is not permission.
- **Mount Vesuvius** — recovered CamStreamer route is not sufficiently tied to a current verified Vesuvius stream. Do not promote by provider reputation alone.
- **Chidori-ga-fuchi Sakura** — seasonal source. The official 2026 Sakura material still exposes a live-camera concept, but September is outside the blossom event context; wait for a fresh seasonal surface before promotion.

These are evidence holds, not missing-code blockers. ERN should prefer a smaller truthful catalog over filling the catalog from historical assumptions.
