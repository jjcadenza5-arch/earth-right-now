# Discovery surfaces

ERN discovery surfaces share truth gates but serve different purposes.

**Watch Earth / Live Right Now** are selective current experiences. They require strict current-source eligibility.

**Explore / ERN AI / search** are broader discovery tools. They normally use actionable discoverable sources. When the visitor explicitly asks for **live**, **current**, **now** or **today**, ordinary search also switches to the strict current-source pool. Currentness words are treated as filters rather than semantic destination terms.

**Living Atlas** remains the broadest actionable network. Its default ordering now prefers genuinely current sources, then legal inside-ERN playback, then source quality. This improves usefulness without pretending the broader Atlas is the same thing as Watch Earth.

**Surprise Me** continues to prefer current sources and legal inside-ERN playback.

None of these ranking choices can change truth, health, permission or playback capability.
