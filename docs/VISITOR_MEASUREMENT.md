# Visitor Measurement Policy

ERN ships with analytics disabled. This is intentional.

Before enabling visitor measurement:

1. Use aggregate, low-data measurement for basic product questions: visits, broad referrers, popular destinations and whether visitors use Watch Earth/Search Earth.
2. Do not send favorites, recent-place history, search text, imported My Earth data or camera-submission contact details to analytics by default.
3. Update the public Privacy page before enabling a provider if its behavior changes the published notice.
4. Keep measurement separate from Earth-view ranking and source truth.
5. Never claim traffic or conversion figures until real production measurement exists.

Current configuration: `provider: NONE`, `enabled: false`.
