# Affiliate post-approval checklist

This checklist begins only after a program sends documented approval.

## For each approved program

1. Record the acceptance date and non-secret evidence reference.
2. Review the current program terms and disclosure requirements.
3. Configure approved tracking credentials or link-generation tools privately.
4. Create one private staging link for a real ERN destination.
5. Verify that the click resolves correctly and attribution is present.
6. Confirm the destination card shows **Affiliate travel option**.
7. Confirm the affiliate relationship does not alter Earth-view ordering, currentness, health or Guide source selection.
8. Enable only a limited public pilot.
9. Watch for broken links, disclosure regressions or tracking failures.

## Never do before approval

- Do not claim a partnership.
- Do not publish tracked links.
- Do not add logos/badges implying acceptance.
- Do not alter Earth-view ranking because of commission.
- Do not commit partner secrets or account credentials to GitHub.
