# Travel Bridge

Travel services are downstream from seeing Earth.

Flow:
1. Visitor sees current conditions.
2. Visitor decides the place is interesting/relevant.
3. ERN may offer nearby stay, food, transport or ticket options.
4. Affiliate/partner links must be clearly distinguished from camera/source attribution.

The camera remains the window and Earth remains the product. Travel monetization must not turn the live viewer into an advertisement wall.
