# Earth Signals privacy notice requirements

Before Earth Signals contribution can be activated, ERN's public privacy notice must plainly describe the feature rather than relying only on internal architecture documents.

The notice must cover: why visitor signals are collected; the structured signal/media types; the 45-minute retention design; that location permission is optional; that public location is place-level rather than precise contributor coordinates; metadata stripping for visitor media; the reporting process; and deletion/expiry behavior.

This contract does not mark the current privacy notice ready. The activation gate's **privacyNotice** capability stays false until the actual public notice contains the required disclosures and the corresponding infrastructure is real.
