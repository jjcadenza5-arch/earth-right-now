# See Before You Go Standard

## Origin

ERN exists to help people **see a place before they go**.

The original strawberry-picking experience exposed the problem clearly: search results and promotional imagery can make a destination look attractive even when the present reality is very different. ERN should reduce that gap by giving visitors a truthful current window.

The lesson is universal. ERN is not only for farms, beaches, landmarks, or any fixed category. **Any place people may genuinely want to visit can belong in ERN if the current visual evidence is useful and good enough.**

## Core product test

Ask:

> **Would this current view help someone understand the place better before deciding whether, when, or how to go?**

If yes, the place is a valid ERN candidate.

## Quality + visitor usefulness

ERN source selection should balance two things together:

1. **Earth-window quality and current usefulness**
   - truthful current/live evidence
   - sufficient visual quality
   - useful view of the place itself
   - reliable enough to help set expectations

2. **Real visitor interest and practical next steps**
   - activities and attractions
   - tickets and culture
   - food and local experiences
   - stays and resorts
   - transport and transfers
   - car/bike/mobility
   - connectivity and other useful travel services

Neither side works alone:
- A commercially useful destination with a poor or misleading camera is not an ERN priority.
- A beautiful camera with little visitor relevance may still belong in Living Atlas, but it may be lower priority for visible curation.
- A strong current window in a place people want to visit is the ideal ERN target.

## Hard gates

No popularity, affiliate opportunity, sponsorship or business relationship bypasses:

1. Truth type is known and honest.
2. Permission is documented or handled fail-closed.
3. Provider/source attribution is retained.
4. Currentness/freshness can be explained.
5. Health and playback are evaluated independently from truth.
6. Promotional, archival or prerecorded media is never substituted for current-condition evidence.

## Research priority

After the hard gates, ERN may prioritize source research using:

- current usefulness / decision value
- visual quality
- visitor interest
- ability to understand the place from the view
- geographic/editorial balance
- provider reliability
- practical travel/commercial fit

Commercial usefulness is a **supporting signal**, not an editorial override. It helps ERN focus effort where visitors are likely to find the live/current view useful and where ERN can also provide practical next steps.

## Broad place universe

ERN may include, among many others:

- cities, streets, plazas and neighborhoods
- beaches, islands and waterfronts
- mountains, ski areas and scenic viewpoints
- farms, orchards and seasonal destinations
- parks, gardens and nature
- zoos, aquariums and animal-viewing locations
- museums, landmarks and cultural attractions
- markets, food districts and shopping areas
- resorts, hotels and destination properties
- transport hubs and gateway cities
- festivals, public spaces and other visitor-interest places

This list is illustrative, not limiting.

## What programs are for

Affiliate and partner programs can help ERN decide **where to invest source-research effort** because they reveal real travel intent and practical visitor needs.

They must never:
- make a weak camera look strong,
- change a truth label,
- buy Watch Earth ranking,
- alter Guide or Stories truth,
- cause ERN to hide better non-commercial places.

## Visitor promise

**See before you go. You see. You decide.**

ERN shows the best truthful current window it can find, then offers practical next steps only when they genuinely fit the place.
