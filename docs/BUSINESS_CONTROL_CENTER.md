# Stage Q — Business Operations Control

Stage Q is an internal control layer, not a visitor-facing dashboard.

Its purpose is to answer five operational questions from one read-only artifact:

1. Which commercial programs and verified offers have real evidence?
2. Is privacy-minimal outbound attribution prepared or actually active?
3. Which distribution channels are really connected?
4. Which participation/business features are still behind deployment or activation gates?
5. What external action is genuinely required next?

## Non-negotiable interpretation rules

A click is not a booking. A verified offer is not revenue. A submitted affiliate application is not acceptance. A partner ID does not imply broad product/API access. A social-channel entry marked NOT_CONNECTED does not imply that an account exists.

Business status never changes:
- source truth;
- camera permission;
- source health;
- playback eligibility;
- Watch Earth ordering;
- ERN Guide source selection;
- Stories ranking;
- editorial curation.

Stage Q may report evidence and external gates. It may not forecast revenue, infer conversion, create partner claims, activate external accounts, or turn commercial activity into editorial rank.
