# ERN recovered whole-product roadmap — 23 September 2026

ERN is not only Watch Earth's Top 20. The public product is a connected system:

1. **Watch Earth / Top 20** — strict, curated, current, beautiful and truthful.
2. **Search / Explore** — visitor-led place search across the broader catalog; destinations are more important than isolated camera records.
3. **Living Atlas** — broad Earth network, including healthy current views, official external sources, sources under recheck, degraded provenance and clearly labeled previews. Atlas is broader than Top 20.
4. **Local Earth / small places** — villages, neighbourhoods, farms, markets, harbours, marinas, small towns and useful non-famous places should be intentionally discoverable.
5. **Business / See → Decide → Go** — after a visitor chooses a place, useful actions can include stay, food, activities, tickets and transport. Affiliate/sponsored relationships must be disclosed and never buy editorial ranking.
6. **Camera-owner submissions** — public camera/source submissions require authority/permission, truth review, health review and conservative embed rights. Submission is never automatic publication.
7. **Earth Signals / Now Moments** — short-lived visitor observations first; later temporary photos/short clips only after moderation, abuse reporting, metadata stripping, rate limits, expiry/deletion and privacy infrastructure are real.
8. **My Earth** — browser-local saved places, recent exploration and light personalization.
9. **Destination pages / discovery** — crawlable place pages and shareable search/place links for Google, AI search and direct sharing.
10. **Multilingual direction** — English, Thai, German, French, Japanese, Chinese and Spanish foundations.
11. **Commercial guardrail** — no paid ranking. Useful Earth discovery comes first.

## Recovered architecture constraints
- Earth → places → windows → moments.
- Live Right Now / Watch Earth is selective; Living Atlas is broad.
- Prefer official/public-interest current views.
- Do not download, restream, crop or obscure third-party branding without permission.
- Link-only when display/embed rights are unclear.
- Visitor contributions never upgrade a source to LIVE/current.
- Monetization is not required for beta launch.
