# Earth Signal moderation and abuse boundary

Earth Signals are lightweight participation, not an open social network.

Structured signals can be eligible for display after normal validation. Visitor photos and clips are **never auto-published**: they require moderation approval and the separate temporary-media safety contract. A reported item is hidden into review rather than kept visible while disputed.

Initial report reasons are wrong place, misleading, privacy, unsafe and spam. ERN should rate-limit contribution and cap active signals per visitor/place before transport is enabled.

No public profiles, follower counts, direct messages, likes or permanent comment history are introduced by this layer.
