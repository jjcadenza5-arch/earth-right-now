# Travel offer trust model

Travel services are downstream from Earth viewing. They must never alter camera truth, health, ranking, or source attribution.

A travel offer is eligible for UI only when:
- it belongs to a valid ERN place context;
- its intent is one of stay, eat, transport, or tickets;
- its destination URL is HTTP(S);
- it has an ID, title and provider;
- it has been explicitly marked verified by ERN's travel-offer review process.

Commercial disclosure is data, not decorative copy:
- sponsored → **Sponsored travel option**
- affiliate → **Affiliate travel option**
- neither → **Travel option**

Camera/source attribution and travel-provider attribution remain separate. An affiliate relationship never grants camera rights and never promotes a source to LIVE.

The live viewer should not become an advertising wall. Travel options belong after the visitor has expressed destination intent.
