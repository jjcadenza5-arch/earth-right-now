# Earth Signals

Earth Signals are short-lived visitor observations attached to an ERN place. They are not permanent reviews and do not prove that a camera is live.

The first contract uses structured observations such as raining, beautiful light, busy, peaceful, something happening, and worth seeing. Signals expire after 45 minutes.

Public ERN surfaces may show the place and whether the observation was verified near that place. They must not expose a contributor's precise coordinates. ERN Guide must describe this layer as visitor reporting rather than verified camera evidence.

Future temporary photos and short clips require separate moderation, abuse reporting, consent, metadata stripping, storage expiry and deletion infrastructure before activation. Visitor media must never upgrade a camera/source to LIVE or current.
