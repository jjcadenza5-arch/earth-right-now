## The visitor journey

Give each post one place or one collection and one next step: curiosity → ERN destination → source view → nearby/planning information. A useful destination page is the promotion asset. Keep the existing site design and commercial neutrality.

Before using a destination, recheck its ERN status, provider attribution, image rights and deep link. Say where the live source opens; do not promise embedded playback for a provider-only view. An archival photo or edited video is a discovery story, not proof of conditions now. Public Guide, Now Moments and other OFF features must not be advertised as available. Use only owner-created or appropriately licensed visual material; public camera availability does not grant reuse rights for its footage.

## Observe before expanding

Record the actual post date, channel and exact entry URL. Capture a dated aggregate baseline, then review after 24 hours, 72 hours and seven days. Existing organic observation records use those windows; this document creates no scheduled task. The latest recorded baseline before this batch was 72 approximate visitors / 559 page views, 39 Facebook-family referral views and 23 Google referral views over 30 days, observed 2026-10-10T12:04:45.628Z. Those historical values must remain dated; obtain a fresh baseline before the next campaign.

Measure referred landings, searches, zero-result searches, window/source opens, completed share actions and explicit travel-option clicks. Consult owner Instagram/Facebook Insights and Google Search Console for reach, impressions and search clicks when available. Compare equal observation windows; rolling totals alone do not establish campaign lift. Referrers may be missing in apps, so some social visits can appear direct. Owner/test activity is not yet separated, and events are not unique people.

Repeat the theme that produces useful exploration, not simply the largest view count. If people arrive but do not explore, check the entry page and invitation. If searches fail, prioritize real destination/alias coverage without inventing cameras. If a source is stale or blocked, repair or label it honestly before promoting it again. Affiliate clicks are interest signals; partner dashboards, not website events, determine eligible bookings and commissions.

Google discovery is a longer-running path: keep useful sourced destination pages, titles, canonical links, sitemap and previews accurate; inspect Search Console rather than claiming rankings from a technical audit. No guaranteed reach, indexing, revenue or market-leading position is implied.
