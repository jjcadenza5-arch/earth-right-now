# Performance Budget

ERN should feel immediate even when the registry becomes large.

- Do not create iframe/video players for cards.
- Only the active shared player loads playable media.
- Cards use lightweight metadata/posters.
- Living Atlas renders compact points, not hundreds of media elements.
- Search/filter operate over in-memory structured data.
- Lazy-load optional imagery.
- Keep homepage curated; broad catalog belongs to Atlas/search.
- Avoid multiple autoplay streams.
