# Earth Signals presentation

Earth Signals are presented as ephemeral visitor reports, never as verified camera evidence.

Visible signals show relative age first (for example, **7 min ago**), their remaining lifetime, and place-level location confidence. A location-supported observation may say **Near this place ✓**; unsupported location must say **Visitor-submitted · location not verified**.

Expired observations are omitted rather than retained as reviews. ERN Guide must continue to attribute this evidence as visitor reporting.

The presentation model contains no seeded visitor activity. ERN must not display fabricated signals, counts, photos or clips merely to make the product look active.
