# Watch Earth curation

Watch Earth is a curated **current Earth** journey, not a generic quality playlist. Its job is to make the world feel alive: when one region is dark or quiet, another part of Earth can be in daylight, golden hour, sunset, or a compelling city night.

The app-level journey requires current verification before any beauty, timing, personalization or diversity scoring.

Rules:
- HEALTHY;
- permission known;
- actionable playback;
- verification still CURRENT_CHECK;
- no PREVIEW;
- solar/time-aware beauty scoring only after truth/currentness gates;
- sunrise, sunset and golden-hour views may rise naturally;
- night views are favored when the subject is appropriate for night, especially city/harbour/skyline contexts;
- geographic and place diversity shape the 20-window journey without overriding eligibility;
- My Earth taste may gently reorder discovery surfaces, but cannot make stale or unavailable material current;
- if the catalog is too small for diversity caps, relax presentation diversity before ever relaxing truth/currentness.

Watch Earth therefore never falls back to stale inventory merely to keep the journey populated. The intended result is simple: **one beautiful moment on Earth is always waiting somewhere.**
