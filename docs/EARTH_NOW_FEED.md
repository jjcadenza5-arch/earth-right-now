# Earth Now feed contract

**Earth is happening** is ERN's future cross-time-zone visitor-signal surface. It is a view over real, unexpired Earth Signals attached to known ERN places.

The feed orders recent evidence by time, keeps visitor provenance, and never manufactures activity to avoid an empty state. When there are no real signals, ERN says that fresh signals will appear when the contribution network is active.

This layer does not activate contribution, storage, visitor counts, media uploads or a social feed. It prepares the presentation contract for the Power of NOW experience while the activation gate remains closed.
