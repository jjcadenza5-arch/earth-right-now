# Primary promotion currentness

PRIMARY is a product-promotion state, not merely a quality score.

A source now needs:
- HEALTHY operational state;
- non-PREVIEW live/current truth;
- known permission;
- CURRENT_CHECK under the source-class recency policy;
- quality >= 70.

A high-quality but stale source remains discoverable in the broader Living Atlas where its status can be shown honestly; it does not receive PRIMARY promotion until reverified.
