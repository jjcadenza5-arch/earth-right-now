# Destination actionability

Destination cards now count actionable windows rather than raw catalog rows.

A place with one healthy window and one offline historical camera is shown as one available window, not "2 windows · choose your view." If no window is actionable, its destination action is disabled and labeled unavailable.

Destination ranking also gives a modest preference to legal inside-ERN playback.

This keeps the destination layer aligned with what a visitor can actually do right now.
