# Earth Signal reporting lifecycle

A report is an operational safety action, not a public reaction or popularity signal.

Valid reports open a review case. While the case is open, the reported Earth Signal should remain hidden. Review can resolve to **REMOVE** or **KEEP**; only a resolved KEEP case can make the item eligible for visibility again, subject to normal expiry and other moderation rules.

Report history is operational data and must not become a public profile, reputation score, comment thread or permanent social history.

This module defines lifecycle semantics only. The activation gate's **reporting** capability remains false until reports can actually be received, stored, reviewed and acted on through real infrastructure.
