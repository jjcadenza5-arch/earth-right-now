# ERN Guide Vision

ERN Guide is a product-wide tour guide, not a generic chatbot.

## Purpose
- Break the ice for new visitors.
- Make ERN understandable without a tutorial.
- Help visitors express vague intent in natural language.
- Turn that intent into exploration across the whole ERN system.

## Guide surfaces
The Guide should be able to hand off into:
- Watch Earth / Top 20
- Search / Explore
- Living Atlas
- Local Earth / small-place discovery
- My Earth
- Now Moments / Earth Signals
- destination pages
- future disclosed travel/business actions

## Example visitor language
- “What is good on Earth right now?”
- “Show me somewhere peaceful.”
- “Take me somewhere with beautiful evening light.”
- “Show me a small local place, not a famous city.”
- “What is near this place?”
- “Is there another view here?”
- “What are visitors seeing around here right now?”
- “Surprise me.”

## Behavior principles
- Use the same currentness, health, permission and truth rules as every other ERN surface.
- Prefer action plus short explanation over long chat.
- Explain “why now” when useful.
- Be warm and guide-like, not verbose or robotic.
- Do not pretend visitor signals are verified camera evidence.
- Do not let affiliate or sponsored status affect Earth-view ranking.
- Preserve multilingual intent handling.
- Help the visitor move; do not trap them in a conversation pane.

## Success condition
A visitor should feel that ERN understands what they are trying to experience, even if they do not know what to search for yet.
