# Destination truth and currentness

Destination-level language must be derived from source-level truth, never from the presence of a HEALTHY flag alone.

A place has a **current window** only when at least one actionable source passes ERN's strict current-source gate: HEALTHY, known permission, CURRENT_CHECK and usable playback.

Destination ranking prefers places with genuinely current sources. Broad Atlas destinations remain discoverable, but stale quality scores cannot make them look more current than verified places.

Visitor labels use the same rule. A stale or rechecking source may say **SOURCE**, **SOURCE CHECK**, **Open source** or **View source**; it cannot inherit **LIVE WINDOW**, **CURRENT AT SOURCE**, **Watch live** or **Open current source** merely from its historical truth type.

This keeps Hero, cards, Choose a Window, destination drawers and Atlas semantics aligned.
