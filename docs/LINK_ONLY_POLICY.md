# Link-only source policy

A public source can be highly valuable to ERN without being embeddable.

**LINK_ONLY** means ERN may curate and describe the official/provider destination and let the visitor open it, but ERN does not copy, proxy, restream or frame the provider's media as an inside-ERN player.

ERN does not infer embed rights from:
- a public URL;
- successful HTTP access;
- an iframe that happens to work technically;
- a site's general footer licence;
- the fact that the provider calls the camera public or live.

Inside-ERN playback requires explicit reviewed permission compatible with ERN's use. Commercial restrictions are treated especially conservatively because ERN may later contain affiliate or advertising features.

Health, truth and permission remain independent. A HEALTHY LINK_ONLY source is a valid current external window, not a failed ERN source.
