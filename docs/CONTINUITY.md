# Earth Right Now — Continuity

## Mission
Earth Right Now (ERN) helps people **See before you go.**

## Product baseline
Fresh is NOT a redesign. The recovered mature ERN lineage is the product specification. Preserve the established light, immersive Earth experience and rebuild fragile implementation underneath it.

## Immutable experience
- Earth/camera is the product; UI stays quiet.
- A visitor can play a selected view where they are. Never force a scroll back to Hero.
- One shared playback controller serves Hero, Watch Earth, Explore, Living Atlas, Moments and My Earth.
- Watch Earth is curated/selective. Living Atlas is broad.
- Multiple high-quality camera choices are valuable.
- Preserve Explore, Watch Earth, Live Right Now, My Earth, Living Atlas/map, Beautiful Moments, ERN AI direction, search/facets, favorites, travel-intent layer and source attribution.
- Old ERN files are recovery/reference material, not production runtime dependencies.

## Source truth
Never call prerecorded content live. Distinguish LIVE VIDEO, LIVE IMAGE, EXTERNAL LIVE, PARTNER and PREVIEW. Health is separate from truth. A legitimate source may be temporarily unavailable.
