# My Earth surface repair

My Earth is now the actual focused destination reached from the Hero.

The redundant legacy `history` surface has been retired. Its source-level "recently opened" list duplicated the more durable My Earth model and encouraged old navigation behavior.

My Earth contains:
- favorite places;
- favorite windows;
- recently visited places.

Place identity survives camera replacement, which is the correct long-term model for ERN.

Opening My Earth does not scroll the page to another player or restore an old scroll coordinate. Focus moves without scrolling.
