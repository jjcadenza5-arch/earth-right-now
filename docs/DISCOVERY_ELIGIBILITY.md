# Discovery eligibility

ERN now has one shared definition for sources that may participate in general discovery.

A discoverable source:
- is not the recovery placeholder;
- is not OFFLINE;
- has an actionable playback capability.

This policy is used by window ranking, general journey selection, nearby discovery and ERN AI ranking. Strict Live Right Now remains narrower and still requires current HEALTHY verification.

The goal is consistency: an unavailable source should not be recommended by one surface while being disabled by another.
