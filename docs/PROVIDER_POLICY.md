# Provider Policy

ERN does not treat every URL the same.

Provider policy determines how much validation is required before promotion:
- video platforms: provider-level live/availability check required;
- official public-agency current-view pages: page health can support Atlas presence, but display/embed rights remain separate;
- unknown providers: conservative UNKNOWN permission until documented;
- EMBED always requires both permission evidence and a functioning embed target.

A 200 HTTP response never proves that a camera is live.
