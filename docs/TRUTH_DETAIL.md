# Truth detail

ERN distinguishes continuous live video from refreshed/current images even when both are useful "right now" travel windows.

Visitor copy may use:
- LIVE VIDEO — continuous live video;
- LIVE IMAGE — current image / refreshed view;
- EXTERNAL LIVE — current/live view at the official provider;
- PARTNER — partner current view;
- PREVIEW — not current.

A LIVE IMAGE card also receives a REFRESHED VIEW badge. This is especially important for National Park Service cameras that update every minute or every 15 minutes rather than streaming continuous video.
