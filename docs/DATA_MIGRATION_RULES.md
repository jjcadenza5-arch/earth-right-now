# ERN Data Migration Rules

Recovered records are evidence, not automatically current truth.

1. Preserve original provider/source URL and recovered verification date.
2. Set health UNKNOWN unless checked again in the current rebuild.
3. Never synthesize an embed URL from a page URL.
4. Preserve link-only restrictions.
5. Multiple cameras at one destination share placeId but retain separate source IDs.
6. Living Atlas may show UNKNOWN health with SOURCE CHECK; primary live surfaces may not call it LIVE.
7. Historical screenshots may guide design but are not production imagery.
8. Never use a browser screenshot as a destination poster.
9. Keep provenance notes for recovered records.
10. A future health worker may promote/demote health without changing truth or permission.
