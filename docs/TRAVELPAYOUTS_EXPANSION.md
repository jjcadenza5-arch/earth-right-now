# Travelpayouts Expansion Research

Status: research and opportunity-routing layer only. No public commercial placement is created by this file.

## Purpose

Earth Right Now should keep Earth discovery first and commercial value downstream. The business layer should not default to Viator/Klook when another already-approved or available Travelpayouts program better matches the visitor's next useful action.

## Current platform facts to apply

- Travelpayouts aggregates many travel-service programs in one platform.
- Relevant service families include accommodation, flights, rail/bus, transfers, car rental, tours/activities, insurance, cruises and other travel services.
- Travelpayouts now automatically connects a Project to programs available for that Project type; some programs are instant-access, some require review, and some may be unavailable based on brand requirements.
- Program-specific terms, reward rates, cookie lifetime and tools must be checked in the owner account before public use.
- Available tools can include deep links, widgets, APIs, data feeds, landing pages, white-label tools and Drive.
- Exact deep links are preferred when they take a visitor to the relevant destination/service rather than a generic homepage.

## ERN routing principle

For each healthy searchable destination, first ask what the visitor may naturally want after seeing the current Earth view:

1. STAY — hotels/resorts/apartments
2. DO — tours, tickets, attractions, activities
3. GET THERE — flights, rail, bus, ferry, transfers
4. MOVE THERE — car rental / local transport
5. PROTECT — travel insurance where genuinely useful
6. SPECIAL — cruises, packages, SIM/Wi-Fi or other destination-specific services

Commercial placement must remain optional and downstream from Earth discovery. One useful action is better than affiliate density.

## Research priority

Audit the owner-side Travelpayouts program catalog and existing ERN project access for suitable programs in these buckets:

### Accommodation
Look for strong hotel/accommodation programs (for example Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia, Rakuten Travel or other currently available programs). Use only exact destination/property links that are genuinely useful.

### Activities
Continue Viator/Klook where appropriate, but also assess GetYourGuide, KKday, Tiqets, Go City, WeGoTrip and other currently available activity/ticket programs.

### Transport
Assess programs for flights, rail/bus, ferries and airport/city transfers. Prioritize island, remote, gateway and multi-modal destinations where transport planning is a meaningful next action.

### Car rental
Assess programs that can help visitors at destinations where independent driving is common or necessary (Iceland, Madeira, Azores, Patagonia, New Zealand, islands, national parks, etc.).

### Insurance
Only use when it is naturally relevant to higher-friction travel and the program/terms permit ERN placement. Never present it as required or use fear-based copy.

### Cruises / packages / extras
Assess only where the destination strongly matches the product (ports, islands, expedition gateways, etc.).

## Safety / truth rules

- Never invent a partner, tracked URL, offer, price, availability or approval.
- Keep ACCOUNT_SEARCH_AND_EXACT_LINK_REQUIRED until the exact owner-side tracked link is available and verified.
- A program being present in Travelpayouts does not mean ERN has access; owner-project availability must be checked.
- Commercial value never affects Earth ranking, source truth, health, Watch Earth eligibility or editorial prominence.
- Prefer one destination-level business bridge serving a cluster over repetitive links on every camera.
- Existing direct/verified partner links remain valid; do not replace them merely for consolidation unless there is a clear business or operational advantage.
