# Mature ERN Feature Inventory

This inventory prevents feature loss during the clean rebuild.

## Primary experience
- Photographic/current-view Hero with location identity and local time.
- Watch live / Watch Earth / My Earth / Explore / Next live.
- Immediate local or immersive playback; never forced scroll to Hero.
- Previous / Next / Source / Full screen / Close controls.
- Multiple camera/window choices where available.

## Discovery
- Explore Right Now.
- Search by place and aliases, including multilingual direction.
- Live, Daylight, Wildlife, Mountains, Sea & Coast, Remote and Surprise Me families/filters.
- Living Atlas / map as broad network discovery.
- Curated Watch Earth journey.
- Beautiful / Interesting / Useful / Happening Now editorial discovery.
- ERN AI direction for intent-led discovery.

## Personal
- My Earth / favorites.
- Recent/history and Wander My Earth / Surprise Me behavior.

## Source/network
- Large underlying Earth catalog; visible front page remains selective.
- Provider/source attribution.
- Rights/permission basis.
- Verification/health/freshness.
- Local time / coordinates / categories / regions.
- Embed and external-live fallbacks.

## Travel bridge
- Nearby places/services and future stay/transport/ticket affiliate layer.
- Business camera submission/partner path.

## Non-regression rule
A clean implementation may reorganize code and navigation, but may not silently remove an item in this inventory.
