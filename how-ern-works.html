HOW ERN WORKS

Earth Right Now — The Live Discovery Engine

See before you go. ERN is a Live Discovery Search Engine that helps people discover what real places around Earth look like now through curated live video, refreshed current images and provider-hosted live sources.

Search → See Live → Discover → Decide → Go

ERN starts with a place or curiosity. It matches that intent to a curated source catalog, keeps source types distinct, and opens the strongest truthful view ERN currently has. Some streams can play inside ERN. Others remain with the official provider and open there.

What ERN verifies

ERN records source/provider identity, source type, health, verification time and—when an embedded player is used—separate playback evidence. A reachable webpage alone is not proof that a camera image is current.

What ERN does not claim

ERN does not own third-party cameras unless explicitly stated, does not relabel stale material as live, and does not guarantee weather, visibility or uninterrupted provider availability. Commercial relationships do not buy editorial ranking.

Public discovery layers

Places contains stable destination pages. Discover groups current/schedule-verified destinations by useful editorial themes. Future ERN Guide, 45-minute Now Moments and additional Local Earth layers remain separately gated until they meet their own truth, privacy and operating requirements.
