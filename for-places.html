Put a real place on Earth Right Now.

ERN is building a reviewed path for public cameras and useful local places. Publication is never automatic, and payment or partner status never buys editorial ranking.

Add your camera

Camera owners should be ready to provide a public camera/source URL, the place name, a contact email, and confirmation that they have authority to submit the source. ERN then reviews truth type, visual quality, travel usefulness, source health and whether embedding is actually permitted. When embed rights are unclear, ERN defaults to linking to the provider rather than assuming permission.

Submission delivery is not open yet.

The review architecture is ready, but ERN will not present a fake working upload form before a real review inbox, privacy/retention policy and submission transport are active.

Local places & small businesses

ERN is not only for famous landmarks. A village, farm, local market, harbour, neighbourhood, beach, marina or small destination can be useful when someone wants to see a place before going there.

Future place participation may include a camera, useful local context, or clearly disclosed planning offers. Commercial relationships remain secondary to the Earth experience: no ranking for sale.

Business side

After a visitor chooses a place, ERN can connect that intent to useful travel actions such as stays, food, activities, tickets and transport. Affiliate or sponsored links must be clearly disclosed and must not determine which Earth window ERN presents as best.
