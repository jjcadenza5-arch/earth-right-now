EDITORIAL PRINCIPLES

Quality over quantity.

Earth Right Now curates useful live/current windows rather than trying to index every camera on Earth. A smaller reliable catalog is more valuable than a large catalog that confuses stale, unavailable or prerecorded material with live reality.

Truth before volume

Source currentness and playback evidence outrank novelty or page count. Reference-only material remains visibly separate from current discovery.

Editorial independence

Affiliate availability, sponsorship or commission level does not determine which Earth view ERN ranks as the best current window. Commercial actions appear only after useful discovery and must be disclosed.

Useful context, not SEO filler

Destination pages expose factual place context, source provenance, freshness information and related ERN destinations. ERN does not generate thin destination pages merely to increase search-engine coverage.

Curiosity and serendipity

ERN can surface famous destinations and small local discoveries. Future serendipitous discovery—the “crispy pork skin principle”—should reveal useful places people did not know to search for, while preserving the same source-quality rules.
