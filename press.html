Earth Right Now

See before you go.

One sentence

Earth Right Now (ERN) helps people look at real places through truthful live and current visual windows before they travel, explore or simply wonder what Earth looks like right now.

Short description

ERN curates verified live video, current images and official external live sources from around the world, then connects them through Watch Earth, search, a living atlas, ERN Guide, Local Earth, Stories and future short-lived visitor moments. Source truth, currentness and permission are kept separate from storytelling, personalization and commercial links.

What makes ERN different

  • Truth first: live video, current image, external live and preview/reference material are not blended into one vague “live” label.
  • The Earth window comes first: travel offers, affiliate links or sponsorship never determine which current view ERN presents as strongest.
  • Curiosity before persuasion: ERN Stories follows the principle “Do not push the answer. Create the question.”
  • Small places matter: villages, farms, harbours, neighbourhoods and local destinations can be useful even when they are not famous.
  • Privacy by default: core exploration works without an account, and local taste stays in the visitor’s browser unless a feature clearly says otherwise.

Collaboration principles

Camera owners and places can prepare sources for ERN review through the For Places & Cameras pathway. Rights, source truth, currentness and playback behavior are reviewed separately. Payment never bypasses review or buys editorial ranking.

Commercial planning links may appear only after a visitor chooses a place, must be clearly disclosed when affiliate or sponsored, and remain separate from Earth-view ranking.

For stories and demonstrations

ERN Stories shows the curiosity-first layer. Now Moments explains the temporary visitor-participation direction. About & Sources explains ERN’s evidence and source principles.

Official account status

ERN does not claim social-media accounts on this page unless they are actually connected and verified. Use earthrightnow.app as the canonical public identity.
