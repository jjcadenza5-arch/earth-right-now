What people are seeing right now.

ERN's visitor-contribution layer is designed to be temporary and place-based, not a permanent review feed or social network.

First: short-lived Earth Signals

Planned structured observations include raining, beautiful light, busy, peaceful, something happening and worth seeing. They expire after about 45 minutes. Precise contributor coordinates are never shown publicly.

Then: temporary photos and short clips

Visitor “Now Moment” photos and short clips remain part of the roadmap, but they will not be activated until ERN has real moderation, abuse reporting, consent, metadata stripping, automatic expiry/deletion, rate limits and an updated privacy notice. Visitor media never upgrades a camera to LIVE or proves that a source is current.

Uploads are intentionally not active yet.

This page explains the product direction without pretending that the required safety and moderation infrastructure already exists.

Preview an Earth Signal

Try the lightweight visitor-expression idea locally. Nothing is uploaded or transmitted.

This is only a browser-local preview. A real public signal would require ERN transport, moderation, rate limits, reporting and expiry infrastructure.

Why this belongs in ERN

Cameras show one fixed window. Temporary visitor moments can eventually reveal what is happening nearby or just outside the frame, while still keeping ERN focused on “See before you go.”
