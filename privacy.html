Privacy at Earth Right Now

Earth Right Now is designed to work without requiring an account. Favorites, recent places, recent windows and interface preferences are stored in your browser unless a feature clearly says otherwise.

ERN links to or embeds third-party camera and travel sources. When you open or play a third-party source, that provider may receive normal web request information and applies its own privacy policy.

The current camera-review draft and Earth Signal preview tools run locally in your browser and do not submit their form contents to ERN. They are readiness tools, not active upload services.

Future generative ERN Guide

ERN is preparing an optional generative Guide, but it is not active yet. Before activation, the Guide must use a secure server-side endpoint so model credentials are never exposed in the browser. The server must rebuild trusted place and source context from ERN's own catalog rather than treating visitor text as source truth.

If the generative Guide is activated later, the intended privacy boundary is: visitor prompts are used only to answer that request; raw prompts and raw model responses are not retained in operational logs; ERN does not build advertising or behavioral profiles from Guide conversations; rate limiting must use a server-derived keyed identifier rather than storing raw network identifiers; and the deterministic Guide remains available when generative processing is unavailable or declined.

The final model/provider-specific data-handling terms must be reviewed and this notice updated if needed before activation. Until then, the public ERN Guide remains deterministic.

ERN does not sell your personal information. If analytics, advertising, accounts, payments or other data-collecting services are added later, this notice must be updated before those features are activated.
